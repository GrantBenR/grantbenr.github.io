UK Spring Break 2026 Trip

feat. WSU Cru, Cap Fam, and Agape

Over my spring break in february of 2026, I decided to go on mission with Central Ohio Cru in the UK. Specifically, we spent most of the trip in Birmingham at and around the University of Birmingham. However, we also spent a day in Leicester at the University of Leicester and a tourist day at the very end in London.

Here's a timeline of the trip:

2/27-2/28: An Unexpected Journey

On Friday afternoon, us cool Wright State folk drove up from Dayton to the John Glenn Airport in Columbus. Once there, we met up with everyone from WSU and with two students from Capital University who will be refered to as "Cap Fam" in future.

From there, we took a short flight to JFK Airport in New York. Our flight got delayed for several hours, but eventually we got going and began our long flight to London. Eventually by late Saturday afternoon, we arrived at London Heathrow airport.
Pictured: Goofing off at JFK Airport in New York.

2/28-3/1: The Bournebrook Inn

From the airport, we took a coach across the British countryside toward Birmingham. We took Ubers from the coach stop to our lodgings for the week at the Bournebrook Inn just outside the University of Birmingham. On Sunday we went to church, visited the University, and met up with Agape staff in Birmingham to plan and prepare for the week.
Pictured: Our group in front of "Big Joe" the tower at the center of the University of Birmingham.

3/2-3/3: Learning to talk to British People

On Monday, we started our on campus ministry. Our group splits in half: one group stays at the campus entrances to table, and the other group pairs up and does initiative evangelism with students all around the campus. On Tuesday, we rinse and repeat tabling and meeting students at UoB.
Pictured: Tabling at the south gate of the University of Birmingham.

3/4: Leicester but not Lesser

On Wednesday, we take the train to Leicester and meet students at the University of Leicester.

3/5: Agape in the Heart of the City

On Thursday, we go to the Birmingham city center and meet up again with Agape staff during the day. While we were there, Joe and I met with a friend I made in Leicester who was coincidentally doing immigration paperwork in Birmingham that day.
Pictured: Myself, Joe, and a friend I made in Leicester who happened to be in Birmingham too that day.

3/5: Reuniting with New Friends

On Thursday night, we return to the university area and host a gathering at a little church that we invite all the people we've met during the week to.
Pictured: Our group standing in front of the little church waiting for someone to bring the keys.

3/6: Obnoxious Americans in the British Capital

On Friday morning, we take a train to London and explore the city for the day.
Pictured: Investigating a phone booth.

3/7: There and Back Again

On Saturday morning, we take the tube back to London Heathrow Airport and begin our flight back to New York then home.
Pictured: Ride to London Heathrow Airport on the tube.
